About Me

Hello, I’m Sergei — a digital service operations and transformation specialist based in the west of Ireland. I work at the intersection of digital systems, service delivery and organisational change.

At the University of Galway, I contribute to digital transformation initiatives including the Blackboard-to-Canvas transition, working across stakeholders, systems integration and service improvement.

My background combines hands-on technical development with experience in automation, workflow design and cross-functional coordination across higher education and digital environments.

I focus on improving how digital capabilities are translated into effective, scalable and user-centred services.
